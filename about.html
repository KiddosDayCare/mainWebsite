Welcome to Our Family

Building foundations for lifelong learning

At Kiddos World Academy, we believe every child deserves a safe, nurturing environment where they can explore, learn, and grow. Our licensed facility in Antioch, CA, provides a warm and professional atmosphere where children thrive.

We understand that choosing the right daycare for your child is one of the most important decisions you'll make. That's why we're committed to providing exceptional care that gives you peace of mind while your little ones enjoy their day of learning and play.

Our commitment: Loving care, professional service, and a foundation for lifelong learning.
