Easy Enrollment Process

Join our family in four simple steps

1

Schedule a Tour

Call us at (925) 595-6280 to schedule a visit. Come see our facility, meet our director, and ask any questions you may have.

2

Complete Enrollment Forms

We'll provide you with all necessary forms including emergency contacts, medical information, and program preferences.

3

Choose Your Program

Select the program and schedule that works best for your family - full-time, part-time, or our Spanish immersion option.

4

Start Your Journey!

We'll help make the transition smooth with a gradual introduction to our routine and environment.
